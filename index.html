Horticulture Learning

Learn Horticulture
Step by Step 🥕🍅🥬

Season: Year-round (Kharif + Rabi)

Popular crops: Tomato, Brinjal, Okra, Cabbage, Cauliflower, Chilli

Key tips: Use raised beds, drip irrigation, organic manure. Protect from frost in winter.

Yield: 20-40 quintal/acre depending on crop

Popular: Mango, Guava, Banana, Papaya, Citrus, Pomegranate

Spacing: Mango 10x10m, Guava 6x6m, Banana 2x2m

Irrigation: Drip preferred. Critical stages: flowering & fruit set.

Schemes: NHB subsidy available for high density planting.

Setup: Polyhouse / Net house recommended

Media: Cocopeat + Vermicompost + Soil (1:1:1)

Care: Maintain 25-30°C, 60-70% humidity. Harden seedlings 7 days before transplant.

Benefits: 40-60% water saving, higher yield, less weed

Subsidy: Up to 55% under PMKSY for small/marginal farmers

Layout: Lateral spacing based on crop (0.6-1.2m for vegetables)

IPM approach: Prefer bio-pesticides (Neem, Trichoderma)

Common pests: Aphids, Whitefly, Fruit borer, Leaf miner

Yellow sticky traps + pheromone traps recommended for monitoring.
